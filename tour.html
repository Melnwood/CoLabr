Five minutes, the whole story.

Co·labr looks like a beautiful wall of updates — but the magic is everything behind it. Here's the tour, front stage and back.

Stop 1 · The composer

Write once, and it looks like you hired a designer

Build each update from blocks — banner photo, story, pictures, video, key numbers, prayer & praise, giving, your personal sign-off. Your organization's real brand colors are built in, a live preview shows exactly what supporters will see, and AI suggests titles people actually open.

Stop 2 · Every heart language

You write in one language. They read in theirs.

Every published update is translated into fourteen languages — and videos get subtitles in all of them, generated before the update even appears. A Czech staff member's grandmother in Brno and their donor in Texas read the very same story, each in the language of their heart.

CZ „A ráno jsem odvezla taťku do nemocnice…"
EN "And this morning I took my dad to the hospital…"
PL „A rano zawiozłam tatę do szpitala…"
14 languages video subtitles too
Stop 3 · A wall for your people only

Your supporters, curated by you

The wall isn't the open internet — it belongs to your support team. You invite people, approve anyone who asks, and every email they receive opens the wall like a key: no passwords, no accounts, nothing for your supporters to remember. Strangers see only a warm "ask to follow."

Stop 4 · Two-way, not broadcast

They pray, write back, and give — one tap away

Under every update your people can tap I'm praying, write you a note, or give — routed through your organization's designated fund. And because everyone on the wall is known, you always know who's standing with you, and every note can be answered.

Stop 5 · Your people, in one place

Invite, import, and actually know your list

Bring supporters over with personal invitations or a CSV from your old tool — including your whole Mailchimp history, photos and all, so you can cancel that subscription. Then watch the picture: who's following and how, who asked to join, who visited the wall this week.

Stop 6 · Safe by default

Nothing sends until you say so

Every new page starts in test mode — write, publish, experiment, import your people, and not a single email goes out until you deliberately flip "Go live." Even then, publishing tells you exactly how many people you're about to reach. No accidental sends, ever. (We learned this one the fun way.)
