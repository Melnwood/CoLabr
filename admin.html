Replay any first-time experience

Use a plus-alias email so nothing real is ever touched, and a private/incognito window so the site forgets you between runs.

  • Outsider joining: private window → colabr.netlify.app → Take a tour → Create your page → sign in with the email option as yourname+out1@gmail.com.
  • JV staff first run: same, but sign in by email as mellenwood+staff1@josiahventure.com — join page, test mode, the works.
  • Supporter's first visit: open your wall with &tourreset=1 added to a supporter link — the welcome walkthrough plays again.
