Test lab
Draft an update in your voice

Say what you want to tell your supporters — ramble, bullet points, half-sentences, anything. It reads your past updates and writes the full update the way you would: your openings, your reflection, your invitation to pray — the facts stay exactly yours. You can steer it too ("keep it short", "more reflective"). Nothing publishes; the draft opens in the composer for your edit.
