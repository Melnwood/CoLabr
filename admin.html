Test lab
Draft an update in your voice

Say what you want to tell your supporters — ramble, bullet points, half-sentences, anything. It reads your past updates and writes the full update the way you would: your openings, your reflection, your invitation to pray — the facts stay exactly yours. You can steer it too ("keep it short", "more reflective"). Nothing publishes; the draft opens in the composer for your edit.

Test drive
Replay any first-time experience

Use a plus-alias email so nothing real is ever touched, and a private/incognito window so the site forgets you between runs.

  • Outsider joining: private window → colabr.netlify.app → Take a tour → Create your page → sign in with the email option as yourname+out1@gmail.com.
  • JV staff first run: same, but sign in by email as mellenwood+staff1@josiahventure.com — join page, test mode, the works.
  • Supporter's first visit: open your wall with &tourreset=1 added to a supporter link — the welcome walkthrough plays again.
Super admins are set by the platform's ADMIN_EMAILS list — right now that's Mel and Noah. Everything on this page acts platform-wide; page-level things (your updates, your people) live on your regular dashboard.
