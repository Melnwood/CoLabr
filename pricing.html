Pricing

Co·labr is being tested right now with a small group of missionary families at Josiah Venture. While we're in this season, there's nothing to buy — we're busy making sure every piece serves real supporters and real stories first.

What pricing will look like

We're shaping the price alongside our testing families, so we won't publish numbers we might break. What we can promise about the shape of it:

Simple. One plan a single missionary can understand and afford — no tiers to decode, no per-email math.

Worth the switch. Co·labr replaces your newsletter tool entirely — writing, translation into fourteen languages, video subtitles, your supporter list, and a private wall your people actually visit — and it's built to cost you less than what you're leaving.

Kind to organizations. When a whole team joins, there'll be an organization plan with your branding across every page and leadership tools included.

Interested? Get in line.

When Co·labr opens past the testing group, we'll send you one email to say it's ready — that's the whole deal. No newsletter, no drip campaign, just the door opening.
