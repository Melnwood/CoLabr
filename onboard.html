The prayer loop — set it up once

Most supporters say they pray and never hear whether it mattered. Co·labr closes that circle for you, and after the first setup it runs on its own.
  1. Write your standing prayer requests. Four categories — mission & vision, our work, family, personal & spiritual. This is what you send when a church writes and asks “do you have any prayer requests?” They live on Dashboard → Prayer requests, and you can press ✦ Draft these from my updates to have them pulled out of what you’ve already written, then edit them into your own words.
  2. Share one link. Your prayer page is a single address you can hand to any church, small group, or prayer team — copy it as text for a bulletin, or print it. Nobody needs an account to read it.
  3. Ask for prayer inside your updates. Any Prayer block you write becomes part of the loop automatically. Supporters tap I’m praying, and Co·labr remembers who prayed for what.
  4. Say what happened. While you’re already writing your next update, Co·labr quietly asks about one older request — God answered, it went another way, or still praying. Everyone who prayed for that exact thing hears back, by name. One question, never a to-do list, and requests retire themselves after 90 days.
  5. Let the month gather itself. On the 1st, everything the month held — what was answered, what still needs prayer, and a fresh way of praying your mission — arrives as a draft update wearing the banner you chose. Read it, change anything, publish or leave it. Nothing goes out in your name without you.
The banner is inherited from last month’s prayer update, so set the picture once and every month matches.
