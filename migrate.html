Bring your history

Pull every newsletter you've ever sent through Mailchimp onto your Co·labr wall — titles, photos, text, dates, and open counts. Your words land in Co·labr's clean design (email layouts don't survive anywhere), and nothing is ever re-emailed to anyone.

1Connect your Mailchimp

Co·labr needs a read key from your Mailchimp account. It takes one minute and the key is used only for this import — never stored:

  1. In Mailchimp, click your profile picture → Account & billing
  2. Open Extras → API keys
  3. Press Create A Key, name it “Co-Labr”, and copy it
