What you get

Everything a newsletter never was

Write once, beautifully

A guided composer — banner, photos, video, numbers, prayer & praise, your personal sign-off. Every update looks like it took a designer.

Every heart language

Write in your own language — readers get theirs. Updates translate into eleven languages automatically, and videos get subtitles.

Supporters choose

Full email, a quick link, a monthly summary, or just the web — each person picks, changes anytime, and leaves gently if they need to.

Giving done right

Your Give button routes to your organization's designated fund for your ministry — receipting and accountability intact.

Getting going

Three steps, no surprises

Create your page

Sign in with Google, name your page, tell us who signs your updates. Two minutes, and your wall exists.

Build and bring your people

Write your first update, import supporters from a CSV or invite them personally — each gets a private link to choose how they'll follow.

Test mode — nothing emails anyone
Go live when you're ready

One deliberate step arms email — it tells you exactly how many people will hear from you. Until then, experiment freely.

Bring your whole organization

When a team joins together, Co-Labr becomes more: your branding on every page, stories featured across the movement, and leadership that actually keeps up with its people through what they write.
