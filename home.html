The gospel has always moved forward through people laboring together.

Those who pray, give, and stand with you don’t just support the work. They share in it. Co·labr keeps you connected, so together you can see and celebrate what God is doing.

What you get

Built for the way missionaries actually write

It looks designed, because the design is already done

Build each update from simple pieces: a banner photo, your story, pictures, video, the numbers that mattered, prayer and praise, and your own sign-off. You write. It comes out looking like somebody laid it out for you.

Write in your heart language. They read in theirs.

Write in your own language. Everything you write reaches every supporter in English, and your national team in their own language. It happens automatically, and videos get subtitles.

Supporters choose

Full email, a quick link, a monthly summary, or just the web. Each person picks, changes anytime, and leaves gently if they need to.

Giving done right

Your Give button routes to your organization's designated fund for your ministry, with receipting and accountability intact.

Getting going

Two steps, no surprises

1 Create your page

Sign in with Google, then answer a few questions. In two minutes, your wall exists.

2 Build and bring your people

Write your first update, import supporters from a CSV or invite them personally. Each one gets a private link, and they choose how they follow:

  • The whole update by email, photos and all
  • Just a short note and a link, for people who prefer a tidy inbox
  • A monthly summary instead of every update
  • A text message when something lands
  • Nothing at all, and read the wall whenever they like

They can change it, or step away gently, from any email you send. You never have to manage anybody's inbox for them.
